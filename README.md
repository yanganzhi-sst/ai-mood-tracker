Page 1 : Journal Tab
1. Navigate to the Journal tab from the sidebar.
2. Type your thoughts and feelings into the journal text box.
3. Select your mood.
4. Click the Save Entry button.
5. A success message will appear once the entry has been saved.

Page 2 : History Tab
1. Navigate to the History tab from the sidebar.
2. All saved journal entries will be shown in order of submission.
3. Each journal entry includes:
- Date and time
- Mood 
- Journal content


Page 3: Self-Care

🧰 SELF-CARE TOOLBOX – Feel Better Now

🌬️ Breathing Tab
Box breathing – Inhale 4, hold 4, exhale 4, hold 4
Slider – Choose how many cycles (2-8)
Progress bar – Shows your breathing exercise progress
Done message – Tells you when it's complete

👁️ Grounding Tab
5-4-3-2-1 exercise – Name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste
Text boxes – Type what you notice

💬 Affirmations Tab
Click for encouragement – Get a random positive message
7 uplifting statements – Different message each time

📝 Prompts Tab
6 journal questions – Ideas when you don't know what to write
