MindEase 🌱
AI-Powered Mood Journal
A private journal that understands your feelings and helps you feel better.

📖 User Guide
📝 PAGE 1: JOURNAL
What to do:

Type in the big text box

Click the green "💾 Save & Analyze Entry" button

Wait for AI analysis (spinning icon)

View your emotion result with emoji

Read self-care tips in 3 columns:

⚡ Quick Relief (actions to do now)

🧠 Mind Shift (ways to think differently)

🫂 Body (physical exercises)

Get a quote and affirmation

Click "📚 See all suggestions" for more tips

🚨 Crisis Detection: If you type concerning words, a red alert appears with Singapore helplines.

📊 PAGE 2: HISTORY
What you'll see:

Stats: total entries, average mood, most frequent emotion

Filter: by date range (All time, Last 7/30 days, This month)

Line chart: mood trend over time

Bar chart: emotion frequency

Recent entries: list with dates and previews

Clear data: option with warning

🧰 PAGE 3: SELF-CARE

Tab	What It Does

👁️ Grounding	5-4-3-2-1 exercise

📝 Prompts	Gentle journal questions
