MindEase 🌱
AI-Powered Mood Journal
A private journal that understands your feelings and helps you feel better.

📖 User Guide
📝 PAGE 1: JOURNAL (Main Page)
What to do:

Type in the big text box (how you're feeling today)

Click the green "💾 Save & Analyze Entry" button

Wait for AI to analyze (spinning icon appears)

View your emotion result with emoji and explanation

Read self-care tips in 3 columns:

⚡ Quick Relief (actions to do now)

🧠 Mind Shift (ways to think differently)

🫂 Body (physical exercises)

Get a quote and affirmation at the bottom

Click "📚 See all suggestions" for more tips

🚨 Crisis Detection: If you type concerning words ("kill myself", "want to die"), a RED alert appears with Singapore helplines.

📊 PAGE 2: HISTORY (Click "📊 History")
What you'll see:

Stats at top: total entries, average mood, most frequent emotion

Filter by date range (All time, Last 7/30 days, This month)

Line chart showing your mood trend over time

Bar chart showing which emotions you feel most often

Recent entries list with dates and previews

Clear data option (with warning)

🧰 PAGE 3: SELF-CARE (Click "🧰 Self-Care")
Four tabs:

Tab	What It Does
🌬️ Breathing	Box breathing timer with adjustable cycles
👁️ Grounding	5-4-3-2-1 exercise (name what you see, feel, hear, smell, taste)
💬 Affirmations	Click for random positive messages
📝 Prompts	List of gentle journal questions
